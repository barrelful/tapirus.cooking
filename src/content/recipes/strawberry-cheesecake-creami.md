Strawberry cheesecake Ninja Creami pint with a dairy-protein base, cooked strawberry ripple, and measured graham crumb.  
Uses part-skim milk, Greek yogurt, reduced-fat cream cheese, whey isolate, erythritol, and sucralose instead of cream-heavy ice cream mix.  
Design intent: one repeatable pint split into two weighed portions with controlled mix-ins and stable re-spin behaviour.

- ⏲️ Prep time: ~25 min
- 🍳 Cook time: ~12 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2 portions (about 286 g each)
- ❄️ Freezer-friendly (freeze base in pint; add ripple and crumb after spinning)

## Ingredients

Cheesecake base
- 250 g part-skim milk, cold
- 100 g plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 55 g reduced-fat cream cheese
- 30 g white chocolate whey isolate or unflavoured whey isolate
- 30 g erythritol
- Sucralose, liquid or powder, equivalent in sweetness to **30 g sugar** (adjust to product strength)
- 5 g lemon juice
- 3 g vanilla extract
- 0.8 g xanthan gum
- 0.5 g fine salt

Strawberry ripple
- 110 g frozen strawberries
- 20 g strawberry jam
- 8 g erythritol
- 5 g lemon juice
- 0.2 g xanthan gum

Cheesecake crumb
- 20 g graham cracker crumbs
- 5 g unsalted butter, melted
- 2 g erythritol

Re-spin adjustment
- 15–30 g part-skim milk, only if needed after the first spin

## Directions

**Cheesecake base**
1. Combine the whey isolate, erythritol, xanthan gum, and salt in a small bowl.
2. Whisk the dry mix thoroughly to disperse the xanthan gum before it contacts liquid.
3. Add the milk, Greek yogurt, cream cheese, lemon juice, vanilla extract, and sucralose to a blender jug.
4. Add the dry mix to the blender jug.
5. Blend for **45–60 seconds** until the cream cheese is fully dispersed and the base is smooth.
6. Rest the base for **5 minutes** to hydrate the xanthan gum.
7. Blend for **10 seconds** to re-homogenise.
8. Pour into one Ninja Creami pint, keeping the surface below the maximum fill line.
9. Freeze on a level shelf for **18–24 hours**.

**Strawberry ripple**
1. Add the frozen strawberries, strawberry jam, erythritol, and lemon juice to a small saucepan.
2. Heat over medium-low heat until the strawberries release liquid and soften, about **4–5 minutes**.
3. Mash the strawberries into small pieces with a spoon or potato masher.
4. Simmer, stirring frequently, until the sauce is reduced to about **70 g**, about **6–8 minutes**.
5. Remove the pan from the heat.
6. Sprinkle the xanthan gum over the hot sauce while whisking constantly.
7. Cool the ripple completely, then refrigerate until the base is spun.

**Cheesecake crumb**
1. Heat the oven to **180 °C**.
2. Combine the graham cracker crumbs, melted butter, and erythritol in a small bowl.
3. Press the mixture into a thin, even layer on a parchment-lined tray.
4. Bake for **5–7 minutes** until lightly toasted.
5. Cool completely.
6. Break into small pieces no larger than **8 mm**.

**Spin and assemble**
1. Run the frozen base on the **Lite Ice Cream** program.
2. If the texture is crumbly or powdery, make a centre well, add **15–30 g part-skim milk**, and run **Re-Spin**.
3. Run one additional **Re-Spin** only if needed for full smoothness.
4. Make a centre well through the spun base.
5. Add the chilled strawberry ripple and cheesecake crumb to the well.
6. Run the **Mix-In** program.
7. Fold once with a spoon only if the ripple remains concentrated in one area.

## Portioning & Storage

**Per portion (ready to eat):**
- 1 portion = **½ finished pint**, about **286 g** after adding ripple and crumb, before any optional re-spin milk.

**Storage**
- Freezer (unspun base only): up to **1 month** before first spin.
- Refrigerator (strawberry ripple): up to **5 days** in a covered container.
- Room temperature (cheesecake crumb): up to **3 days** in an airtight container if fully cooled and dry.
- Freezer (finished pint): up to **2 weeks** after adding ripple and crumb; re-spin leftovers only if the full pint can be returned level to the machine.
- Serving from frozen leftovers: temper **10–15 minutes** in the refrigerator, or re-spin the full pint if the surface is level.

## Notes

- The base removes cream and relies on whey isolate, Greek yogurt, reduced-fat cream cheese, and xanthan gum for body.
- White chocolate whey isolate fits the cheesecake profile; unflavoured isolate gives more control but may need a small increase in sucralose.
- Erythritol is capped at **40 g per pint** across all components to limit cooling sensation and crystallisation risk.
- Sucralose supplies sweetness without adding water or bulk carbohydrate, while erythritol contributes some freezing-point depression.
- The strawberry ripple keeps **20 g jam** for pectin, dissolved solids, and a less icy fruit phase.
- Reducing the ripple to **70 g** concentrates strawberry flavour and prevents excess free water from weakening the spun base.
- The crumb is deliberately small and low-butter compared with a cheesecake crust; it is a texture accent, not a separate crust layer.
- Add ripple and crumb only after the first spin because freezing them inside the base increases iciness and reduces Creami blade efficiency.
- Nutrition assumes part-skim milk at about **2% fat**, 0% Greek yogurt, reduced-fat cream cheese, and a typical whey isolate.
