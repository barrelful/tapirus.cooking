Salted caramel Ninja Creami ice cream built from a measured dark caramel, skim milk, whey protein, and one egg yolk.  
Uses reduced sugar, erythritol, and xanthan gum instead of the original cream-heavy custard base.  
Design intent: one repeatable pint with controlled caramel flavour, high protein density, and stable re-spin behaviour.

- ⏲️ Prep time: ~15 min
- 🍳 Cook time: ~10 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2 portions (about 250 g each)
- ❄️ Freezer-friendly (freeze base in pint; portion after spinning)

## Ingredients

Caramel custard base
- 35 g granulated sugar
- 20 g water
- 40 g water, for deglazing
- 410 g skim milk, cold, divided
- 1 large egg yolk (**18 g**)
- 0.8 g fine salt

Protein base
- 30 g vanilla or white chocolate whey protein powder
- 25 g erythritol
- 0.9 g xanthan gum
- 5 g vanilla extract

Re-spin adjustment
- 15–30 g skim milk, only if needed after the first spin

## Directions

**Caramel custard base**
1. Add the granulated sugar and water to a small heavy saucepan.
2. Heat over medium heat until the sugar dissolves and the syrup reaches a steady boil.
3. Stop stirring once the syrup boils.
4. Cook, swirling the pan as needed, until the caramel is deep amber, about **178–182 °C**, and smells toasted but not burnt.
5. Remove the pan from the heat.
6. Add the deglazing water in **2–3 small additions**, stirring carefully after each addition because the mixture will bubble aggressively.
7. Return the pan to low heat and stir until any hardened caramel dissolves into a smooth syrup.
8. Add cold skim milk gradually, stirring constantly, until the caramel milk reaches **58–60 °C**.
9. Stir in the salt.
10. Whisk the egg yolk in a small bowl until smooth.
11. Whisk **30–50 g** of the hot caramel milk into the yolk to temper it.
12. Whisk the tempered yolk back into the saucepan.
13. Heat gently to **64–68 °C** and hold for **3–4 minutes**, stirring constantly and scraping the base and corners of the pan.
14. Remove from the heat immediately.
15. Add the remaining cold skim milk and stir until the mixture drops below **40 °C**.

**Protein base**
1. Combine the whey protein, erythritol, and xanthan gum in a bowl.
2. Whisk the dry mix thoroughly to disperse the xanthan and reduce clumping.
3. Add the vanilla extract to the cooled caramel custard base.
4. Blend with an immersion blender while adding the dry mix gradually.
5. Continue blending for **30–45 seconds** after the last dry addition, until fully smooth.
6. Rest the mixture for **5 minutes** to hydrate the xanthan.
7. Blend for **10 seconds** to re-homogenise.
8. Pour into one Ninja Creami pint, keeping the surface below the maximum fill line.
9. Freeze on a level shelf for **12–24 hours**.

**Spin**
1. Run the pint on the **Lite Ice Cream** program.
2. If the texture is crumbly or powdery, make a centre well, add **15–30 g skim milk**, and run **Re-Spin**.
3. Run one additional **Re-Spin** only if needed for full smoothness.

## Portioning & Storage

**Per portion (ready to eat):**
- 1 portion = **½ pint**, about **250 g** after spinning, before any optional re-spin milk.

**Storage**
- Freezer (unspun pint): up to **1 month** before first spin.
- Freezer (after spinning): transfer individual half-pint portions to covered containers and freeze up to **1 month**.
- Serving from frozen leftovers: temper **10–15 minutes** in the refrigerator, or re-spin if returning the full pint to the machine.

## Notes

- The caramel is kept at **35 g sugar** to preserve cooked caramel flavour while avoiding the full sugar load of a conventional custard ice cream.
- Yield assumes the initial caramel water and most deglazing water are lost as steam during caramel cooking and dissolution.
- Deglazing hot caramel with room-temperature water is hazardous. Use a saucepan with substantial spare volume and a long utensil; if unsure, let the caramel cool below **100 °C** before adding the deglazing water.
- Skim milk replaces cream and whole milk; whey protein and egg yolk restore body and emulsification with fewer calories from fat.
- The custard is a modified crème anglaise: one yolk is diluted across a larger low-fat milk base, so **64–68 °C** gives useful thickening with manageable curdling risk when stirred constantly.
- Xanthan gum is used at about **0.2%** of mix mass to bind free water and reduce iciness in the low-fat base.
- Cold temperatures mute sweetness and caramel aroma, but the caramel, whey powder, and erythritol provide enough sweetness without high-intensity sweetener.
