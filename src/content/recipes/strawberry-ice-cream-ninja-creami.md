Strawberry Ninja Creami ice cream with a blended dairy-protein base, jam-supported fruit flavour, and measured fruit load.  
Uses 2% milk, 0% Greek yogurt, skim milk powder, whey isolate, erythritol, and sucralose instead of cream and high sugar.  
Design intent: one repeatable pint split into two weighed portions with stable Lite Ice Cream re-spin behaviour.

- ⏲️ Prep time: ~10 min
- 🍳 Cook time: ~0 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2 portions (about 260 g each)
- ❄️ Freezer-friendly (freeze base in pint; portion after spinning)

## Ingredients

Base
- 125 g 2% milk, cold
- 75 g plain 0% Greek yogurt
- 200 g frozen strawberries, thawed
- 30 g unflavoured whey isolate
- 25 g erythritol
- 40 g skim milk powder
- Sucralose, liquid or powder, equivalent in sweetness to **20 g sugar** (adjust to product strength)
- 15 g strawberry jam
- 5 g lemon juice
- 3 g vanilla extract
- 1.0 g xanthan gum
- 0.5 g fine salt

Re-spin adjustment
- 15–30 g 2% milk, only if needed after the first spin

## Directions

**Base**
1. Combine the whey isolate, erythritol, skim milk powder, xanthan gum, and salt in a small bowl.
2. Whisk the dry mix thoroughly to disperse the xanthan gum before it contacts liquid.
3. Add the 2% milk, Greek yogurt, thawed strawberries, strawberry jam, lemon juice, vanilla extract, and sucralose to a blender jug.
4. Add the dry mix to the blender jug.
5. Blend for **45–60 seconds** until the strawberries are fully dispersed and the base is smooth.
6. Rest the base for **5 minutes** to hydrate the xanthan gum.
7. Blend for **10 seconds** to re-homogenise.
8. Pour into one Ninja Creami pint, keeping the surface below the maximum fill line.
9. Freeze on a level shelf for **18–24 hours**.

**Spin**
1. Run the frozen base on the **Lite Ice Cream** program.
2. If the texture is crumbly or powdery, make a centre well, add **15–30 g 2% milk**, and run **Re-Spin**.
3. Run one additional **Re-Spin** only if needed for full smoothness.
4. Level the surface before returning any leftover ice cream to the freezer.

## Portioning & Storage

**Per portion (ready to eat):**
- 1 portion = **½ finished pint**, about **260 g** after spinning, before any optional re-spin milk.

**Storage**
- Freezer (unspun pint): up to **1 month** before first spin.
- Freezer (after spinning): transfer individual half-pint portions to covered containers and freeze up to **2 weeks**.
- Serving from frozen leftovers: temper **10–15 minutes** in the refrigerator, or re-spin only if the full pint can be returned level to the machine.

## Notes

- Whey isolate and skim milk powder raise protein density without the fat load of cream.
- Greek yogurt adds dairy solids and acidity; the lemon juice sharpens strawberry flavour at frozen serving temperature.
- Skim milk powder is high at **40 g per pint**; it increases lactose and milk solids for body but may add a light cooked-milk note.
- Strawberry jam adds pectin, sugar, and concentrated strawberry flavour more efficiently than increasing raw strawberry alone.
- Erythritol is limited to **25 g per pint** to reduce cooling effect and crystallisation risk.
- Sucralose supplies the remaining sweetness without adding water or bulk carbohydrate.
- Xanthan gum is required for this low-fat formula; omitting it will produce a colder, icier texture after freezing.
- Thawing the strawberries before blending prevents large frozen pieces from reducing blender efficiency and improves pint homogeneity.
- Nutrition assumes standard 2% milk, plain 0% Greek yogurt, commercial skim milk powder, standard strawberry jam, and a typical unflavoured whey isolate.
