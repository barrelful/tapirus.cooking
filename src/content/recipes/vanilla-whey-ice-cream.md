Vanilla-focused Ninja Creami ice cream built around whey protein, part-skim milk, and one egg yolk.  
Uses erythritol, sucralose, and xanthan gum to keep calories controlled while maintaining body in a low-fat base.  
Design intent: one repeatable pint with stable re-spin behaviour and clear half-pint portion control.

![Vanilla whey ice cream in a Ninja Creami pint](/pix/vanilla-whey-ice-cream.webp)

- ⏲️ Prep time: ~10 min
- 🍳 Cook time: ~0 min
- 🍽️ Servings: 2 portions (about 241 g each)
- ❄️ Freezer-friendly (freeze base in pint; portion after spinning)

## Ingredients

Base
- 400 g part-skim milk
- 30 g white chocolate whey protein powder (MyProtein)
- 1 large egg yolk (**18 g**)
- 25 g erythritol
- Sucralose, liquid or powder, equivalent in sweetness to **12 g sugar** (adjust to product strength)
- 1.0 g xanthan gum
- 8 g vanilla extract
- 0.5 g fine salt

## Directions

**Base**
1. Combine the whey protein, erythritol, xanthan gum, and salt in a bowl.
2. Whisk the dry mix thoroughly to disperse the xanthan and reduce clumping.
3. Add the milk, egg yolk, vanilla extract, and sucralose to the Ninja Creami pint or a blender jug.
4. Add the dry mix and blend **30–45 seconds** until completely smooth.
5. Rest the mixture **5 minutes** to allow the xanthan to hydrate.
6. Pour into one Ninja Creami pint, level the surface, and freeze on a flat shelf for **12–24 hours**.

**Spin**
1. Run the pint on the **Lite Ice Cream** program.
2. If the texture is crumbly, make a small centre well, add **15–30 g milk**, and run **Re-Spin**.
3. Run one additional **Re-Spin** only if needed for full smoothness.

## Portioning & Storage

**Per portion (ready to eat):**
- 1 portion = **½ pint**, about **241 g** after spinning.

**Storage**
- Freezer (unspun pint): up to **1 month** before first spin.
- Freezer (after spinning): transfer individual half-pint portions to covered containers and freeze up to **1 month**.
- Serving from frozen leftovers: temper **10–15 minutes** in the refrigerator or re-spin if returning the full pint to the machine.

## Notes

- The egg yolk may be pasteurised before use. Seal the yolk in a zip-top bag and hold at **58–60 °C** in a water bath for **30–60 minutes**; lower temperatures require longer holds. Cool before blending.
- One yolk improves emulsification and body without the calorie cost of cream.
- Xanthan gum stabilises the low-fat base and reduces iciness after the first spin and any later re-spins.
- Split sweetening keeps bulk sweetness from erythritol while allowing final sweetness adjustment with sucralose.
- Vanilla extract is intentionally high relative to batch size because cold serving temperatures mute aroma.
- Nutrition assumes negligible calories from the sucralose product and includes erythritol within total carbohydrate.
